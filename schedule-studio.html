01 / THE CHALLENGE

Start with the operational friction.

Overnight work, employee-specific Peak overrides, exceptions, and attendance complicate day-to-day planning.

02 / THE SOLUTION

A workflow built around the task.

A local scheduling workspace separates planned shifts from attendance and supports reviewable reports and exports.

  • Employee rosters and shift definitions
  • Regular schedules and employee-specific Peak schedules
  • Calendar editing, copying, and attendance records
  • Print, Excel, and PNG exports with backup and restore
03 / EVIDENCE & OUTCOME
Personally used and tested

Personally used by the developer to set up normal and Peak-season schedules.

The developer reports using the application at work for actual normal and Peak schedules. A prior supplied review reported 68 of 68 included automated tests passing for the reviewed build; those tests were not rerun here.

Evidence basis: developer experience, supplied review notes, and application captures.

04 / CONSTRAINTS & SAFETY

Clear limits are part of the design.

Personal workplace use does not establish organization-wide rollout. Local network sharing has not been established as deployment-tested, and current-release qualification remains separate.

05 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Problem definition through iteration.

I identified the workflow problem, specified desired behavior, used AI-assisted coding tools to develop and iterate, and evaluated outputs. Application-specific use and testing are described above.
