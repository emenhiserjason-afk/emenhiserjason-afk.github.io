MY CONTRIBUTION

My approach to
AI-assisted development.

I start with the problem, not the code.

My background in IT infrastructure and technical operations gives me firsthand experience with complicated systems, repetitive workflows, and the practical challenges employees face. I use that experience to recognize opportunities for better tools.

These projects show how I apply more than 25 years of IT experience to a newer way of developing useful software.

  1. Define the problem. I work out how the solution should behave and establish the operational and technical requirements.
  2. Direct the implementation. I use AI-assisted coding tools to help turn the design into working software.
  3. Test and refine. I review results, test workflows, identify problems, and continue refining the application around the intended need.

My contribution combines domain knowledge, technical judgment, solution design, AI-assisted implementation, testing, and iteration.
